# Alpha - 0.2.146 [RU](/ru/changelogs/146)/[EN](/en/changelogs/146)

## Bugfixes/Improvements

* **[Movement]** Fixed movement arrival detection. Rebuilding a route no longer resets spent stuck-recovery attempts.
* **[AutoPickup]** Once attempts to approach an item are exhausted, the bot stops repeating the same approach and returns to other actions, including Follow.
* **[AutoStash]** A failed stash approach no longer restarts indefinitely or prevents Follow from continuing.
* **[AutoFollow]** A temporary gap in the party roster no longer clears evidence of the leader's departure or spent transition and teleport attempts.
